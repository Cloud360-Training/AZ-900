# Practice & Assessment

Testing your knowledge before taking the actual AZ-900 exam is crucial for success. This section provides links to official practice assessments and community-recommended resources.

## 📝 Official Microsoft Practice Resources

Microsoft provides free resources to help you gauge your readiness for the exam.

### 1. Free Practice Assessment
* **Link**: [Take a free Practice Assessment](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/exams/az-900/practice/assessment?assessment-type=practice&assessmentId=23)
* **Overview**: This official practice assessment tests your skills with questions similar to those you will encounter on the actual exam. It provides immediate feedback and explanations for correct and incorrect answers.

### 2. Exam Sandbox
* **Link**: [Explore the Exam Sandbox](https://aka.ms/examdemo)
* **Overview**: Familiarize yourself with the exam user interface before test day. The sandbox allows you to experience the look and feel of the exam environment, including how to navigate between questions, mark items for review, and submit your exam.

## 📚 Community Recommended Practice Tests

While the official assessment is the best starting point, many candidates find value in additional practice tests to ensure comprehensive coverage of all edge cases.

### 1. Tutorials Dojo Practice Exams
* **Link**: [AZ-900 Practice Exam Questions](https://tutorialsdojo.com/az-900-microsoft-azure-fundamentals-sample-exam-questions/)
* **Overview**: Tutorials Dojo is highly regarded in the cloud certification community for providing accurate and challenging practice questions with detailed explanations.

### 2. Udemy Practice Tests
* **Link**: [Search Udemy for AZ-900 Practice Tests](https://www.udemy.com/courses/search/?src=ukw&q=az-900+practice+tests)
* **Overview**: Udemy hosts numerous practice test courses. Look for highly-rated courses with recent updates (ensure they reflect the January 2026 exam changes).

## 💡 Exam Tips

1. **Read Carefully**: Pay close attention to keywords like "most cost-effective," "least administrative effort," or "requires no infrastructure management."
2. **Process of Elimination**: If you are unsure of an answer, eliminate the obviously incorrect options first.
3. **Understand the "Why"**: Don't just memorize answers; understand why an answer is correct and why the others are wrong.
4. **Review the Study Guide**: Regularly review the [official study guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/resources/study-guides/az-900) to ensure you are comfortable with all "Skills Measured."
